# Printer Management

To replace a paper roll or print a test page, navigate to the `Printer` tab in the election manager menu and select `Load Paper` or `Print Test Page`.

<figure><img src="../.gitbook/assets/vxscan-em-printer-tab.png" alt="VxScan election manager Printer tab with Load Paper and Print Test Page buttons" width="375"><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

If loading a new paper roll, the screen will walk you through removing the paper roll holder and loading a new paper roll. Select `Print Test Page` to print a test page and confirm the paper was loaded properly.

<div><figure><img src="../.gitbook/assets/vxscan-em-printer-remove-roll-holder.png" alt="VxScan Remove Paper Roll Holder dialog with instructions for opening the access door" width="375"><figcaption></figcaption></figure> <figure><img src="../.gitbook/assets/vxscan-em-printer-load-new-roll.png" alt="VxScan election manager Load New Paper Roll dialog with loading instructions" width="375"><figcaption></figcaption></figure> <figure><img src="../.gitbook/assets/vxscan-em-printer-paper-detected.png" alt="VxScan election manager Paper Detected dialog offering to print a test page" width="375"><figcaption></figcaption></figure></div>

{% hint style="info" %}
The paper roll can be replaced at any point of time, with the machine turned on or off. Using the election manager printer flow is not required but can be helpful for someone who needs step-by-step instructions or wants to print a test page.
{% endhint %}

To replace or add a printer paper roll:

* [ ] Open the poll worker door
* [ ] Open the access door
* [ ] Press the release lever on the right side of the paper roll holder
* [ ] Remove the paper roll holder

<div><figure><img src="../.gitbook/assets/photo-vxscan-printer-open-panels.png" alt="VxScan with the lid open and numbered arrows showing the two panels to open to reach the printer roll"><figcaption><p>Open panels</p></figcaption></figure> <figure><img src="../.gitbook/assets/photo-vxscan-printer-push-release-lever.png" alt="Finger pressing the green release lever on the end of the VxScan printer roll holder"><figcaption><p>Push the release lever</p></figcaption></figure> <figure><img src="../.gitbook/assets/photo-vxscan-printer-roll-holder-remove.png" alt="Hand lifting the paper roll holder out of the VxScan printer"><figcaption><p>Remove the paper roll case</p></figcaption></figure></div>

{% hint style="warning" %}
The paper roll must be installed in the correct orientation or it will not print. The correct orientation is indicated on the side of the paper roll holder.
{% endhint %}

* [ ] Place one end of the paper roll on the paper roll holder
* [ ] Snap the other end of the paper roll onto the paper roll holder
* [ ] Pull a small amount of paper up and over the tear bar

<div><figure><img src="../.gitbook/assets/photo-vxscan-printer-roll-place-one-end.png" alt="Hands placing one end of a new paper roll onto the VxScan roll holder"><figcaption><p>Place one end on the case</p></figcaption></figure> <figure><img src="../.gitbook/assets/photo-vxscan-printer-roll-snap-other-end.png" alt="Hands snapping the other end of the paper roll into the VxScan roll holder"><figcaption><p>Place the other end on the case</p></figcaption></figure> <figure><img src="../.gitbook/assets/photo-vxscan-printer-pull-paper-over-tear-bar.png" alt="Hands holding the paper roll holder above the VxScan printer with paper pulled up over the tear bar"><figcaption><p>Pull paper through the feed</p></figcaption></figure></div>

* [ ] Snap the paper roll holder back into VxScan
* [ ] Remove excess paper by pulling toward you and tearing

<div><figure><img src="../.gitbook/assets/photo-vxscan-printer-roll-holder-reinsert.png" alt="Hands snapping the loaded paper roll holder back into the VxScan printer" width="188"><figcaption><p>Snap case back in to VxScan</p></figcaption></figure> <figure><img src="../.gitbook/assets/photo-vxscan-printer-tear-excess-paper.png" alt="Hand tearing excess paper off the VxScan printer after reinserting the roll holder" width="188"><figcaption><p>Remove excess paper</p></figcaption></figure></div>

* [ ] Close the access door
* [ ] Close the poll worker door
