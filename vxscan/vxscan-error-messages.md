# VxScan Error Messages

VxScan is programmed to intuitively explain to a voter or poll worker problems that may occur with a ballot or the VxScan. The following is a list of potential error messages, in alphabetical order, and the actions a voter or a poll worker should take to remedy the error.

Ballots must be printed full-scale.</td><td>Ask a poll worker for help.</td><td>Contact your election administrator for properly printed ballots.</td><td><p>eventId: scanner-state-machine-transition<br>message: Will include "rejecting"<br><br>Preceding scanner-state-machine-event log will include "invalid_scale" in its message</p></td></tr><tr><td>Ballot Scan Failed - There was a problem scanning your ballot. \*Error messages with an asterisk next to them correspond to unexpected error states where the cause is not immediately known. VotingWorks will likely ask for logs after the election to identify the cause and improve the product for future elections.

\*\*Error messages with a double asterisk next to them are meant to aid technicians assembling machines. They are not expected to be seen in the field.

{% hint style="info" %}
VxScan's ballot box capacity is 3000 ballot sheets. If the ballot box reaches capacity, ballots should be removed and secured according to state law and rule.
{% endhint %}
