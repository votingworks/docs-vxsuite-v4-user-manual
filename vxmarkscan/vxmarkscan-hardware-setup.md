# VxMarkScan Hardware Setup

## VxMarkScan Setup

Part of the Precinct System, VxMarkScan is a free-standing accessible ballot marking device that gives voters the ability to mark their choices and print their ballot from a touchscreen.

**VxMarkScan** can be set up through the following steps:

**Set up the stand**:

* [ ] With the legs facing up, raise a leg until it snaps in place
* [ ] Continue until all legs are raised
* [ ] Flip the stand up right

<div><figure><img src="../.gitbook/assets/photo-vxmarkscan-stand-raise-leg.png" alt="Hand raising one leg of the folded VxMarkScan stand until it snaps in place" width="188"><figcaption><p>Raise leg to snap in place</p></figcaption></figure> <figure><img src="../.gitbook/assets/photo-vxmarkscan-stand-one-leg-raised.png" alt="The VxMarkScan stand on the floor with one leg raised and snapped into place." width="188"><figcaption></figcaption></figure> <figure><img src="../.gitbook/assets/photo-vxmarkscan-stand-all-legs-raised.png" alt="VxMarkScan stand base with all four legs raised and snapped into place" width="188"><figcaption><p>All legs in place</p></figcaption></figure></div>

* [ ] Snap the support bar in place on the back two legs

<div><figure><img src="../.gitbook/assets/photo-vxmarkscan-stand-support-bar.png" alt="The VxMarkScan stand support bar lying on the floor before installation" width="375"><figcaption><p>Support bar</p></figcaption></figure> <figure><img src="../.gitbook/assets/photo-vxmarkscan-support-bar.png" alt="Hand snapping the support bar onto the back legs of the VxMarkScan stand." width="188"><figcaption><p>Snap support bar to back legs</p></figcaption></figure> <figure><img src="../.gitbook/assets/photo-vxmarkscan-stand-support-bar-installed.png" alt="VxMarkScan stand with the support bar snapped onto the two back legs" width="188"><figcaption><p>Support bar installed</p></figcaption></figure></div>

* [ ] Add power block holder to the back center on the top of the stand
* [ ] Insert power block into the holder so cords lay out each side

<div><figure><img src="../.gitbook/assets/photo-vxmarkscan-power-block-holder.png" alt="Hand holding the power block holder above the slotted center of the VxMarkScan stand." width="188"><figcaption><p>Add power block holder</p></figcaption></figure> <figure><img src="../.gitbook/assets/photo-vxmarkscan-power-block-in-holder.png" alt="Power block seated in the holder on the back center of the VxMarkScan stand" width="188"><figcaption><p>Insert power block into the holder</p></figcaption></figure> <figure><img src="../.gitbook/assets/photo-vxmarkscan-stand-power-block-installed.png" alt="Assembled VxMarkScan stand with the power block holder and cords installed" width="188"><figcaption><p>Power block and holder installed</p></figcaption></figure></div>

**Set up the ballot marking device:**

* [ ] Using two people, lift and place the ballot marking device on the stand by aligning the stand bars with the bottom

<div><figure><img src="../.gitbook/assets/photo-vxmarkscan-main-unit.png" alt="Top view of the VxMarkScan main unit showing the accessible controller, screen and printer-scanner cover." width="375"><figcaption><p>Ballot marking device</p></figcaption></figure> <figure><img src="../.gitbook/assets/photo-vxmarkscan-unit-on-stand.png" alt="Rear view of the VxMarkScan ballot marking device placed on its stand with power cord routed below" width="375"><figcaption><p>Place ballot marking device on the stand</p></figcaption></figure></div>

* [ ] Plug in the power cord connector to the back of the ballot marking device
* [ ] Plug the power cord into the APC UPS battery backup

<div><figure><img src="../.gitbook/assets/photo-vxmarkscan-power-cord-connector.png" alt="Hand holding the round power cord connector, marked TOP, beneath the VxMarkScan unit." width="188"><figcaption><p>Power cord connector</p></figcaption></figure> <figure><img src="../.gitbook/assets/photo-vxmarkscan-power-cord-connector-plugged-in.png" alt="Power cord connector plugged into the underside of VxMarkScan beside the power button and headphone jack." width="375"><figcaption><p>Power cord connector plugged in</p></figcaption></figure> <figure><img src="../.gitbook/assets/photo-vxmarkscan-power-plug.png" alt="Hand holding the three-prong AC plug of the VxMarkScan power cord beneath the unit." width="188"><figcaption><p>Power plug</p></figcaption></figure> <figure><img src="../.gitbook/assets/photo-vxmarkscan-power-cord-plugged-into-ups.png" alt="VxMarkScan power cord plugged into an outlet on the APC Back-UPS 850 battery backup." width="188"><figcaption><p>Power cord plugged in</p></figcaption></figure></div>

* [ ] Push the printer-scanner cover release button in
* [ ] Open the printer-scanner cover
* [ ] Slide the ballot bin into place
* [ ] Close the printer-scanner cover
* [ ] Document and seal the ballot bin (loop on right side) as required

<div><figure><img src="../.gitbook/assets/photo-vxmarkscan-push-printer-cover-button.png" alt="Hand pressing the printer cover release button on the underside of the VxMarkScan" width="188"><figcaption><p>Push the printer cover button</p></figcaption></figure> <figure><img src="../.gitbook/assets/photo-vxmarkscan-printer-cover-open.png" alt="VxMarkScan printer cover open, exposing the thermal printer with its green push-to-close label" width="188"><figcaption><p>Open the printer cover</p></figcaption></figure> <figure><img src="../.gitbook/assets/photo-vxmarkscan-ballot-bin.png" alt="The VxMarkScan ballot bin standing on its own with its lid closed." width="188"><figcaption><p>Ballot bin</p></figcaption></figure> <figure><img src="../.gitbook/assets/photo-vxmarkscan-ballot-bin-attached.png" alt="Ballot bin slid into place on the side of the VxMarkScan with the printer cover held open" width="188"><figcaption><p>Slide ballot bin into place</p></figcaption></figure> <figure><img src="../.gitbook/assets/photo-vxmarkscan-ballot-bin-seal.png" alt="Close-up of the seal loop where the ballot bin meets the VxMarkScan body" width="188"><figcaption><p>Document and seal as required</p></figcaption></figure></div>

* [ ] Place the privacy shield pegs in the holes on each side of the ballot marking device

<div><figure><img src="../.gitbook/assets/photo-vxmarkscan-privacy-shield-pegs.png" alt="Privacy shield panels laid flat with the four yellow pegs circled in blue" width="375"><figcaption><p>Privacy shield pegs</p></figcaption></figure> <figure><img src="../.gitbook/assets/photo-vxmarkscan-privacy-shield-holes.png" alt="Two privacy shield peg holes on the side of the ballot marking device circled in blue" width="375"><figcaption><p>Privacy shield holes</p></figcaption></figure> <figure><img src="../.gitbook/assets/photo-vxmarkscan-privacy-shield-installed.png" alt="VxMarkScan on its stand with the privacy shield installed around the screen, controller and printer." width="375"><figcaption><p>Privacy shield installed</p></figcaption></figure></div>

* [ ] Plug the headphones into the front headphone jack
* [ ] Push the power button to turn VxMarkScan on

<div><figure><img src="../.gitbook/assets/photo-vxmarkscan-headphones-plugged-in.png" alt="Headphones resting beside the accessible controller with their cable plugged into the front headphone jack." width="188"><figcaption><p>Plug the headphones in</p></figcaption></figure> <figure><img src="../.gitbook/assets/photo-vxmarkscan-power-button.png" alt="Blue arrow pointing to the power button on the underside of VxMarkScan, next to the plugged-in power connector." width="375"><figcaption><p>Push the power button to turn on</p></figcaption></figure></div>

The VxMarkScan is set up.

## Cleaning VxMarkScan

The scanner should be cleaned for every election with scanner cleaning solution (see [supply-list.md](../miscellaneous/supply-list.md "mention")) and a lint-free or microfiber cloth.

* [ ] Press printer cover button to open printer cover
* [ ] Open scanner by lifting the metal piece above the green sticker
* [ ] Apply cleaning solution to cloth and wipe the inside of the scanner with moderate force
  * [ ] Clean scanner glass
  * [ ] Clean rollers
* [ ] Close scanner by pressing down on the green sticker until the scanner clicks into place
* [ ] Close printer cover until it clicks into place
