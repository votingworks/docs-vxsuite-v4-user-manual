# Packing Up VxMarkScan

Pack up **VxMarkScan** through the following steps:

1. **Pack up the ballot marking device**:

* [ ] Push the power button to turn off. VxMarkScan has shutdown properly once the screen is off and presents no content.
* [ ] Unplug and store the headphones
* [ ] Remove and store the privacy shield

<div><figure><img src="../.gitbook/assets/photo-vxmarkscan-power-button.png" alt="Blue arrow pointing to the power button on the underside of VxMarkScan, next to the plugged-in power connector." width="188"><figcaption><p>push power button to turn off</p></figcaption></figure> <figure><img src="../.gitbook/assets/photo-vxmarkscan-headphones-plugged-in.png" alt="Headphones resting beside the accessible controller with their cable plugged into the front headphone jack." width="188"><figcaption><p>unplug and store headphones</p></figcaption></figure> <figure><img src="../.gitbook/assets/photo-vxmarkscan-privacy-shield-installed.png" alt="VxMarkScan on its stand with the privacy shield installed around the screen, controller and printer." width="188"><figcaption><p>remove and store privacy shield</p></figcaption></figure></div>

* [ ] Push the printer-scanner release button to open the cover
* [ ] Lift the ballot bin to remove

<div><figure><img src="../.gitbook/assets/photo-vxmarkscan-push-ballot-bin-release.png" alt="Hand pressing the ballot bin release on VxMarkScan with the printer-scanner cover open."><figcaption></figcaption></figure> <figure><img src="../.gitbook/assets/photo-vxmarkscan-remove-ballot-bin.png" alt="Hand lifting the ballot bin out of VxMarkScan with the printer-scanner cover open."><figcaption></figcaption></figure> <figure><img src="../.gitbook/assets/photo-vxmarkscan-ballot-bin.png" alt="The VxMarkScan ballot bin standing on its own with its lid closed."><figcaption></figcaption></figure></div>

* [ ] Unplug the power cord from the APC UPS battery backup
* [ ] Unplug the power cord connector from the back of the ballot marking device

<div><figure><img src="../.gitbook/assets/photo-vxmarkscan-power-cord-plugged-into-ups.png" alt="VxMarkScan power cord plugged into an outlet on the APC Back-UPS 850 battery backup."><figcaption></figcaption></figure> <figure><img src="../.gitbook/assets/photo-vxmarkscan-power-plug.png" alt="Hand holding the three-prong AC plug of the VxMarkScan power cord beneath the unit."><figcaption></figcaption></figure> <figure><img src="../.gitbook/assets/photo-vxmarkscan-power-cord-connector-plugged-in.png" alt="Power cord connector plugged into the underside of VxMarkScan beside the power button and headphone jack."><figcaption></figcaption></figure> <figure><img src="../.gitbook/assets/photo-vxmarkscan-power-cord-connector.png" alt="Hand holding the round power cord connector, marked TOP, beneath the VxMarkScan unit."><figcaption></figcaption></figure></div>

* [ ] Remove and store the ballot marking device from the stand
* [ ] Remove and store the power block from the holder
* [ ] Remove and store the power block holder

<div><figure><img src="../.gitbook/assets/photo-vxmarkscan-main-unit.png" alt="Top view of the VxMarkScan main unit showing the accessible controller, screen and printer-scanner cover."><figcaption></figcaption></figure> <figure><img src="../.gitbook/assets/photo-vxmarkscan-power-block.png" alt="The VxMarkScan power block with its DC connector cable and AC power cord laid out on the floor."><figcaption></figcaption></figure> <figure><img src="../.gitbook/assets/photo-vxmarkscan-power-block-holder.png" alt="Hand holding the power block holder above the slotted center of the VxMarkScan stand."><figcaption></figcaption></figure></div>

2. **Pack up the stand:**

* [ ] Remove the support bar
* [ ] Flip the stand over
* [ ] With the legs facing up, pull a leg toward you and fold in
* [ ] Continue until all legs are folded down

<div><figure><img src="../.gitbook/assets/photo-vxmarkscan-support-bar.png" alt="Hand snapping the support bar onto the back legs of the VxMarkScan stand."><figcaption></figcaption></figure> <figure><img src="../.gitbook/assets/photo-vxmarkscan-pull-stand-leg.png" alt="Hand pulling a leg of the VxMarkScan stand up from its folded position."><figcaption></figcaption></figure> <figure><img src="../.gitbook/assets/photo-vxmarkscan-stand-one-leg-raised.png" alt="The VxMarkScan stand on the floor with one leg raised and snapped into place."><figcaption></figcaption></figure> <figure><img src="../.gitbook/assets/photo-vxmarkscan-stand-folded.png" alt="The VxMarkScan stand lying flat on the floor with all four legs folded down."><figcaption></figcaption></figure></div>

3. **Pack all components into their cases**

* [ ] Pack the BMD case
  * [ ] BMD (main unit)
  * [ ] Power brick
  * [ ] Headphones
* [ ] Pack the peripherals case
  * [ ] Legs
  * [ ] Power brick holder
  * [ ] Ballot box
  * [ ] Privacy shield
