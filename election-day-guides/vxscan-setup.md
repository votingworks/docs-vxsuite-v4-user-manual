# VxScan Setup

**VxScan** has three primary components: the scanner in a hard case, the collapsible ballot box, and a Goldenmate 1000VA/600W uninterruptible power supply (UPS).

<figure><img src="../.gitbook/assets/photo-vxscan-components-packed.jpg" alt="Closed VxScan case next to the collapsed ballot box with its telescoping handle raised" width="375"><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

## VxScan Setup

\
**VxScan** can be set up through the following steps:

1. **Set up the ballot box:**
   * [ ] Release the straps on each side
   * [ ] Secure the straps on the underside of the lid

<div><figure><img src="../.gitbook/assets/photo-vxscan-ballot-box-collapsed-straps-closed.jpg" alt="Collapsed VxScan ballot box from the side with the strap fastened and the handle extended" width="188"><figcaption><p>Straps when closed</p></figcaption></figure> <figure><img src="../.gitbook/assets/photo-vxscan-ballot-box-fasten-strap-to-lid.jpg" alt="Hand hooking the purple strap buckle onto the storage hook on the underside of the ballot box lid" width="188"><figcaption><p>Fastening strap to lid</p></figcaption></figure> <figure><img src="../.gitbook/assets/photo-vxscan-ballot-box-tighten-strap-to-lid.jpg" alt="Purple strap buckle seated on the lid hook with the strap pulled tight" width="188"><figcaption><p>Tightening strap to lid</p></figcaption></figure> <figure><img src="../.gitbook/assets/photo-vxscan-ballot-box-lid-straps-stored.jpg" alt="Underside of the VxScan ballot box lid with both straps hooked and stored" width="188"><figcaption><p>Straps for set up</p></figcaption></figure></div>

* [ ] Pull the box open and gently push the purple hooks down with your foot

<div><figure><img src="../.gitbook/assets/photo-vxscan-ballot-box-expanded-lid-open.jpg" alt="Expanded VxScan ballot box seen from the side with the lid lifted open" width="375"><figcaption><p>Box fully expanded</p></figcaption></figure> <figure><img src="../.gitbook/assets/photo-vxscan-ballot-box-expanded-lid-closed.jpg" alt="Expanded VxScan ballot box from the side with the lid closed on top" width="375"><figcaption><p>Box interior when expanded</p></figcaption></figure></div>

* [ ] Close the lid, pushing until you hear a click

<div><figure><img src="../.gitbook/assets/photo-vxscan-ballot-box-expanded-top-view.jpg" alt="Looking down into the open, expanded VxScan ballot box with the lid folded aside" width="375"><figcaption><p>Lifting the lid</p></figcaption></figure> <figure><img src="../.gitbook/assets/photo-vxscan-ballot-box-lifting-lid.jpg" alt="Hand lifting the VxScan ballot box lid, showing the underside with its strap hooks" width="375"><figcaption><p>Lid clicked into place</p></figcaption></figure></div>

* [ ] Seal the ballot box as required

2\. **Set up the scanner:**

* [ ] Place the case on top of the ballot box by lining up the bottom with the opening on the ballot box and slide the case back
* [ ] Push the button to release and open each latch to open the case lid

<div><figure><img src="../.gitbook/assets/photo-vxscan-case-on-ballot-box.jpg" alt="Closed VxScan case seated on top of the expanded ballot box" width="375"><figcaption><p>Case slid onto ballot box</p></figcaption></figure> <figure><img src="../.gitbook/assets/photo-vxscan-full-setup.jpg" alt="VxScan case open on top of the expanded ballot box, screen off, poll worker door and ballot box door closed" width="375"><figcaption><p>Case opened on ballot box</p></figcaption></figure></div>

* [ ] Open the poll worker door (upper left corner)
* [ ] Push the purple security bolt down to secure the case to the ballot box
* [ ] Close the poll worker door and seal and document as required

<div><figure><img src="../.gitbook/assets/photo-vxscan-security-bolt-secure.jpg" alt="Thumb pushing the purple security bolt down inside the VxScan poll worker door to secure the case" width="188"><figcaption><p>Pressing the security bolt down</p></figcaption></figure> <figure><img src="../.gitbook/assets/photo-vxscan-security-bolt-sealed.jpg" alt="Close-up of the VxScan case security bolt pressed down with a green numbered tamper seal threaded through it." width="188"><figcaption><p>Sealing the poll worker door</p></figcaption></figure></div>

* [ ] Remove the power cord from its storage place
* [ ] Plug the power cord into the back of the scanner and then into the Goldenmate 1000VA/600W UPS

<div><figure><img src="../.gitbook/assets/photo-vxscan-case-back-cord-wrapped.jpg" alt="Back of the VxScan case on the ballot box with the power cord wrapped in its storage recess" width="375"><figcaption><p>Wrapped power cable</p></figcaption></figure> <figure><img src="../.gitbook/assets/photo-vxscan-case-back-cord-plugged-in.jpg" alt="Back of the VxScan case with the power cord unwrapped and plugged into the power inlet" width="375"><figcaption><p>Power cable plugged into scanner</p></figcaption></figure></div>

* [ ] Plug the UPS into an outlet
* [ ] Turn the UPS on
* [ ] Seal and document the ballot box door as required

<figure><img src="../.gitbook/assets/photo-vxscan-ballot-box-door-sealed.jpg" alt="Close-up of the purple VxScan ballot box door latch secured with a green tamper seal." width="188"><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

{% hint style="warning" %}
Be sure to assemble VxScan in an area of the room that allows for voter privacy as voted ballots are inserted into the scanner.
{% endhint %}

## Auxiliary Bin

VxScan has an auxiliary bin that can be used for holding voted ballots when necessary. Most often this would be used in an emergency when the tabulator is not accepting ballots.

To open the auxiliary bin, push the handle down and pull the bin towards you. Stop at the mid-way point. Seal the auxiliary bin when in use.

<div><figure><img src="../.gitbook/assets/photo-vxscan-auxiliary-bin-location.png" alt="Fully assembled VxScan with a blue arrow pointing to the auxiliary bin handle on the ballot box door." width="188"><figcaption></figcaption></figure> <figure><img src="../.gitbook/assets/photo-vxscan-auxiliary-bin-seal-highlighted.png" alt="Front of the VxScan ballot box with a blue box highlighting the sealed auxiliary bin latch." width="188"><figcaption></figcaption></figure> <figure><img src="../.gitbook/assets/photo-vxscan-auxiliary-bin-insert-ballot.png" alt="A hand inserting a ballot into the VxScan auxiliary (emergency) bin slot on the front of the ballot box." width="188"><figcaption></figcaption></figure></div>

To verify the bin is empty or to remove the ballots from the auxiliary bin, push the handle down and pull the bin all the way towards you.
