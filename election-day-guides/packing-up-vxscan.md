# Packing Up VxScan

Pack up **VxScan** through the following steps:

1. **Pack up the scanner**:

* [ ] Insert the poll worker card and select `Power Down` to turn VxScan off. VxScan has shutdown properly once the screen is off and presents no content.

<figure><img src="../.gitbook/assets/vxscan-pw-power-down.png" alt="VxScan poll worker menu with polls closed and the Power Down button highlighted" width="375"><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

* [ ] Once the screen is off, remove the power cord from VxScan
* [ ] Disconnect the power cord from the Goldenmate 1000VA/600W UPS
* [ ] Unplug the UPS from the power outlet
* [ ] Turn off the UPS
* [ ] Pack up the UPS
* [ ] Wrap the power cord in its storage space

<figure><img src="../.gitbook/assets/photo-vxscan-power-cord-storage.png" alt="Hand holding the plug end of the power cord wrapped in the storage recess on the back of the VxScan case" width="375"><figcaption><p>Remove and wrap the power cord</p></figcaption></figure>

* [ ] Push the purple release button to the left to release the case from the ballot box
* [ ] Close the poll worker door
* [ ] Close the case lid and latch
* [ ] Seal and document the case seal(s) as required
* [ ] Pull the case forward and lift the case off of the ballot box

<div><figure><img src="../.gitbook/assets/photo-vxscan-security-bolt-release.jpg" alt="Thumb pushing the purple security bolt release inside the VxScan poll worker door" width="188"><figcaption><p>Push the security bolt release</p></figcaption></figure> <figure><img src="../.gitbook/assets/photo-vxscan-case-latches.png" alt="Two hands working the latches on the closed VxScan case lid while it sits on the ballot box" width="188"><figcaption><p>Close the case lid, latch, and seal</p></figcaption></figure> <figure><img src="../.gitbook/assets/photo-vxscan-slide-case-onto-ballot-box.png" alt="Hands sliding the closed VxScan case into the recess on top of the ballot box" width="188"><figcaption><p>Pull the case off the ballot box</p></figcaption></figure></div>

2. **Pack up the ballot box:**

* [ ] Unseal and/or unlock the ballot box
* [ ] Remove all ballots from the ballot box and seal into a secure container, recording the seal number as required
* [ ] Press the latch on the lid to release the lid and lift the lid over to the side

<div><figure><img src="../.gitbook/assets/photo-vxscan-ballot-box-press-lid-latch.jpg" alt="Hand pressing the purple latch on top of the VxScan ballot box lid" width="188"><figcaption><p>Press lid latch</p></figcaption></figure> <figure><img src="../.gitbook/assets/photo-vxscan-ballot-box-lift-lid.jpg" alt="Hand lifting the VxScan ballot box lid open by its latch" width="188"><figcaption><p>Lift lid</p></figcaption></figure> <figure><img src="../.gitbook/assets/photo-vxscan-ballot-box-expanded-lid-open.jpg" alt="Expanded VxScan ballot box seen from the side with the lid lifted open" width="375"><figcaption><p>Set lid to the side</p></figcaption></figure></div>

* [ ] Fully collapse the ballot box by gently using your foot to lift the bottom purple hooks

<div><figure><img src="../.gitbook/assets/photo-vxscan-ballot-box-foot-hook.jpg" alt="Foot lifting the purple hook at the bottom of the VxScan ballot box to collapse it" width="563"><figcaption><p>Using the foot hook</p></figcaption></figure> <figure><img src="../.gitbook/assets/photo-vxscan-ballot-box-collapsing.jpg" alt="VxScan ballot box partly collapsed, seen from above with the vinyl walls folding inward" width="563"><figcaption><p>Collapsing the ballot box</p></figcaption></figure> <figure><img src="../.gitbook/assets/photo-vxscan-ballot-box-collapsed.jpg" alt="VxScan ballot box fully collapsed, standing on its wheels with the lid alongside" width="375"><figcaption><p>Box when collapsed</p></figcaption></figure></div>

* [ ] Remove the straps from the underside of the lid and tighten them around the side

<div><figure><img src="../.gitbook/assets/photo-vxscan-ballot-box-remove-strap-from-lid.jpg" alt="Hand unhooking a purple strap buckle from the storage hook on the underside of the ballot box lid" width="188"><figcaption><p>Remove straps from lid</p></figcaption></figure> <figure><img src="../.gitbook/assets/photo-vxscan-ballot-box-fasten-strap.jpg" alt="Hand hooking the purple strap buckle onto the side bracket of the collapsed ballot box" width="188"><figcaption><p>Fasten straps around side</p></figcaption></figure> <figure><img src="../.gitbook/assets/photo-vxscan-ballot-box-tighten-strap.jpg" alt="Hand pulling the strap tight through the purple buckle on the collapsed ballot box" width="188"><figcaption><p>Tighten straps</p></figcaption></figure> <figure><img src="../.gitbook/assets/photo-vxscan-ballot-box-strapped.jpg" alt="Collapsed VxScan ballot box with the strap fastened and tightened around its side" width="188"><figcaption><p>Ballot box secured</p></figcaption></figure></div>

{% hint style="info" %}
The scanner must be stored securely in a climate-controlled environment. The ballot box can be stored wherever convenient.
{% endhint %}
