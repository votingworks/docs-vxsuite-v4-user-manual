# Seal Recommendations

### VxAdmin, VxCentralScan, & VxPrint

While in storage, VxAdmin, VxCentralScan and VxPrint should be stored in their cases with seals through each of the eyelets to the right and left of the carrying handle on the case. Reseal and document as required each time the case is opened and repacked for storage.

### VxScan

**In Storage:** The scanner should be sealed with seals through each of the eyelets to the right and left of the carrying handle on the case.

**During Voting:**&#x20;

* A seal should be placed through the hole in the security bolt to keep the poll worker door closed and the scanner latched to the ballot box.
* A seal should be placed through the hasp in the auxiliary bin door when it is not in use.
* A seal should be placed through the hasp in the front ballot door.

### VxMarkScan

**In Storage:** A sticker seal should be placed between the top and bottom of the case.

**During Voting:** A seal should be placed through the eyelets holding the ballot box in place.

### VxMark

**In Storage:** The device should be sealed with seals through each of the eyelets to the right and left of the carrying handle on the case.

**During Voting:** A seal should be used to seal the slider over the USB port in a closed position.
