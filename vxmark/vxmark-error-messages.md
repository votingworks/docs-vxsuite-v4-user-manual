# VxMark Error Messages

VxMark is programmed to intuitively explain to a voter or poll worker problems that may occur with a ballot or the VxMark. The following is a list of potential error messages and the actions a voter or a poll worker should take to remedy the error.

<table><thead><tr><th>Error Message</th><th width="178.744873046875">Voter Action</th><th width="189.13787841796875">Poll Worker Action</th><th>Log File Entry</th></tr></thead><tbody><tr><td>No Printer Detected</td><td>Ask a poll worker for help.</td><td>If polls are open, insert a poll worker card to silence the alert, then connect the printer to resume voting. Otherwise, connect the printer to continue.</td><td>eventId: printer-config-removed<br>message: "The previously configured printer is no longer detected."</td></tr><tr><td>Internal Connection Problem**</td><td>Ask a poll worker for help.</td><td>Reboot VxMark by holding the power button on the back left of the machine until the screen goes black. Press the button again to turn it back on. If the error persists after restarting, ask your election official to contact VotingWorks support.</td><td>No specific log entry.</td></tr><tr><td>Card Backwards</td><td>N/A</td><td>Remove the smart card, turn it around, and insert it again.</td><td>eventId: auth-login<br>disposition: failure<br>reason: card_error</td></tr><tr><td>Invalid Card - Remove the card to continue.</td><td>N/A</td><td>Try inserting the card again. If the problem persists, contact your election administrator for a new poll worker card.</td><td>eventId: auth-login<br>disposition: failure<br>reason: Many possible values</td></tr><tr><td>Number of &#x3C;limitType> in &#x3C;electionEntity> (&#x3C;value>) exceeds VxMark system limit of &#x3C;limitValue>.</td><td>N/A</td><td>N/A</td><td>eventId: election-configured<br>disposition: failure</td></tr><tr><td>Something went wrong - Ask a poll worker to restart the ballot marking device.*</td><td>Ask a poll worker for help.</td><td>Restart the machine. If problems persist after restarting, ask your election official to contact VotingWorks support.</td><td>eventId: unknown-error</td></tr></tbody></table>

\*Error messages with an asterisk next to them correspond to unexpected error states where the cause is not immediately known. VotingWorks will likely ask for logs after the election to identify the cause and improve the product for future elections.

\*\*Error messages with a double asterisk next to them are meant to aid technicians assembling machines. They are not expected to be seen in the field.
