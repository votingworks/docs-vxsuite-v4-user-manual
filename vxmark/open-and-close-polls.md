# Open and Close Polls

## Open Polls on VxMark

After VxMark is turned on, the screen will show "Polls Closed":

<figure><img src="../.gitbook/assets/vxmark-unauthenticated-polls-closed.png" alt="Polls Closed screen asking to insert a poll worker card to open" width="188"><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

Verify the election name, election date, and polling place are correct in the bottom left corner.

{% hint style="warning" %}
If there is an orange callout that says "Test Ballot Mode" on the screen, contact your election administrator immediately to prepare the machine for the election.
{% endhint %}

**In order to open the polls:**

* [ ] Insert the poll worker card into the card reader in the back right
* [ ] Select `Open Polls`
* [ ] Confirm by selecting `Open Polls` again
* [ ] Verify the number of ballots printed is zero
* [ ] Remove the poll worker card

<div><figure><img src="../.gitbook/assets/vxmark-pw-menu-polls-closed.png" alt="Poll Worker Menu with polls closed showing Open Polls and Power Down buttons" width="188"><figcaption></figcaption></figure> <figure><img src="../.gitbook/assets/vxmark-pw-open-polls-modal.png" alt="Open Polls confirmation dialog with the Open Polls button highlighted" width="188"><figcaption></figcaption></figure> <figure><img src="../.gitbook/assets/vxmark-pw-menu-polls-open.png" alt="Poll Worker Menu with polls open showing Close Polls and Pause Voting buttons" width="188"><figcaption></figcaption></figure></div>

The polls are now open and voting can begin.

## Close Polls on VxMark

* [ ] Insert the poll worker card into the card reader in the back right
* [ ] Select `Close Polls`
* [ ] Confirm by selecting `Close Polls` again
* [ ] Remove the poll worker card

<div><figure><img src="../.gitbook/assets/vxmark-pw-menu-polls-open-close-polls-highlighted.png" alt="VxMark poll worker menu with polls open and the Close Polls button highlighted"><figcaption></figcaption></figure> <figure><img src="../.gitbook/assets/vxmark-pw-close-polls-modal.png" alt="VxMark poll worker menu with the Close Polls confirmation dialog and the Close Polls button highlighted."><figcaption></figcaption></figure> <figure><img src="../.gitbook/assets/vxmark-pw-menu-polls-closed.png" alt="Poll Worker Menu with polls closed showing Open Polls and Power Down buttons"><figcaption></figcaption></figure></div>

The polls are now closed and voting is complete.

## Pause or Resume Voting

The `Pause Voting` action is generally used by jurisdictions conducting early voting over multiple days or during an emergency situation. To pause voting and not allow ballots to be marked by VxMark:

* [ ] Insert the poll worker card into the card reader in the back right
* [ ] Select `Pause Voting`
* [ ] Confirm by selecting `Pause Voting` again
* [ ] Remove the poll worker card

<div><figure><img src="../.gitbook/assets/vxmark-pw-menu-pause-voting-highlighted.png" alt="Poll Worker Menu with polls open and the Pause Voting button highlighted"><figcaption></figcaption></figure> <figure><img src="../.gitbook/assets/vxmark-pw-pause-voting-modal.png" alt="Pause Voting confirmation dialog with the Pause Voting button highlighted"><figcaption></figcaption></figure> <figure><img src="../.gitbook/assets/vxmark-pw-menu-polls-paused.png" alt="Poll Worker Menu with voting paused showing Resume Voting and Close Polls buttons"><figcaption></figcaption></figure></div>

Voting is now paused. When you want to resume voting:

* [ ] Insert the poll worker card into the card reader in the back right
* [ ] Select `Resume Voting`
* [ ] Confirm by selecting `Resume Voting` again
* [ ] Remove the poll worker card

<div><figure><img src="../.gitbook/assets/vxmark-pw-menu-resume-voting-highlighted.png" alt="VxMark poll worker menu with voting paused and the Resume Voting button highlighted" width="375"><figcaption></figcaption></figure> <figure><img src="../.gitbook/assets/vxmark-pw-resume-voting-modal.png" alt="Resume Voting confirmation dialog with the Resume Voting button highlighted" width="375"><figcaption></figcaption></figure></div>

Voting can now continue.
